About WopCart

An independent cart for independent stores.

WopCart exists so independent WordPress stores can sell digital products, licenses and subscriptions without surrendering their store to a platform.

Who runs WopCart

WopCart.com is run by C. Badde, a sole trader based in Mellieħa, Malta. The full company details are in the imprint.

Mission

Independent WordPress stores should be able to sell digital products, licenses and subscriptions on their own site, under their own brand, without handing the store to a platform.

Principles

What WopCart
stands for.

Four rules that shape the plugin and the pricing.

GPL plugin

WopCart is free software released under the GPL.

Your data stays on your site

Your store runs in your own WordPress installation, so your store data stays with you.

All features on every plan

Plans differ by production domains and payment routing, not by features.

Transparent pricing

The free plan charges a 2% WopCart fee per transaction; paid plans charge 0%. The free plan opens with Stripe Connect onboarding, which is not live yet. See pricing.
