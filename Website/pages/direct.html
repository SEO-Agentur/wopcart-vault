WopCart Direct

Your own payment accounts. No platform fee.

The free plugin settles payments through WopCart and keeps 2% of each sale. WopCart Direct swaps that route for your own gateway accounts and takes nothing. It is an add-on: you install it next to the free plugin, nothing is replaced, and if you remove it your store keeps selling at 2%.

What the add-on changes

Four things, and nothing else. Every feature of the shop is already in the free plugin.

0% WopCart fee

Instead of 2% on every transaction. Your payment provider's own fees become your only transaction cost, and the money never passes through us.

Five gateways, your accounts

Stripe, PayPal, Authorize.net, Mollie and 2Checkout with your own credentials, plus Apple Pay through your own Stripe account. Payouts arrive on your provider's schedule, not ours.

One licence key

It belongs to the add-on alone. The free plugin never asks for a key and never will — a plugin in the WordPress.org directory has to work without one.

Updates for the add-on

Delivered from here, signed and tied to your key. The free plugin keeps updating through WordPress.org, paid customers included.

Three sizes

Every size has all features, a 0% WopCart fee and your own gateway accounts. They differ in the number of production domains you may activate. Yearly renews automatically; lifetime is one payment with no renewal.

What happens if the licence lapses

Your gateways keep charging. A licence that expires or is cancelled does not switch your payment accounts off, does not reinstate the 2% fee and does not touch a single order. The one thing that stops is the delivery of add-on updates, until you renew.

That is deliberate. Software you have paid for should not stop working because an invoice did, and a shop should never go dark over a renewal date. The key exists to secure the download and to count production domains — nothing in the checkout depends on it.

Removing the add-on altogether is just as uneventful: your settings stay, your orders stay, and payments route back through WopCart at 2% on the next sale. Details on plan changes and refunds.
