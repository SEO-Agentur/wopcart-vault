EU VAT for digital products sold from WordPress

WopCart applies the VAT rate of the buyer's country, validates VAT numbers with VIES, applies the reverse charge for EU business buyers and keeps the evidence and invoices you need.

This page describes software features. It is not tax advice.

VAT rates by country

  • VAT rates looked up for the buyer's EU country
  • A tax class per product: standard rate for digital goods, reduced rate, e-publication rate, or not taxable
  • Your store country decides which rules apply to you as the seller

Evidence of the customer's location

EU rules for digital services require evidence of where the customer is. WopCart stores the billing country and the country derived from the buyer's IP address with each order, and records whether they agree.

To get the IP-based country, the IP address is sent to a geolocation service (ipapi.co) when EU VAT is enabled. The result is cached.

VAT numbers and the reverse charge

  • VAT numbers entered at checkout are validated against the European Commission's VIES service. You can turn this off.
  • Valid cross-border B2B sales can be zero-rated under the reverse charge
  • The invoice then states: "Reverse charge: VAT to be accounted for by the recipient under Article 196 of Council Directive 2006/112/EC."

Invoices and credit notes

  • Sequential invoice numbers per series
  • Invoices are built from a snapshot taken at the time of sale, so later product or address changes do not alter them
  • A refund creates a separately numbered credit note
  • Optional connection to InvoicesGenerator for hosted PDFs

Reports for your OSS return

  • VAT collected per country, the figures an OSS return asks for
  • Revenue, product and gateway reports alongside

Digital content and the right of withdrawal

EU consumers can usually withdraw from an online purchase within 14 days. For digital content delivered immediately, the right ends only if the buyer expressly agrees to immediate delivery and acknowledges losing the right.

WopCart adds this consent to checkout (on by default) and stores the time of consent with the order.

Currencies and personal data

  • Prices shown in the visitor's currency with automatic exchange rates
  • Structured data always uses your base-currency prices
  • GDPR erasure anonymizes personal data but keeps invoice records that tax law requires you to retain

VAT questions

Does WopCart file my OSS return?

No. WopCart calculates VAT at checkout and reports VAT per country. You or your accountant file the return.

Is VAT reverse charged or just set to zero?

For a valid VAT number from another EU member state, the sale is treated as a reverse charge and the invoice carries the reverse-charge wording.

Can I turn off VIES validation?

Yes. It is a setting under Taxes & VAT.
