Selling

Cart and checkout

  • Checkout, confirmation and account pages created on activation
  • Hosted Stripe Checkout (SCA ready) and an on-page Apple Pay button
  • PayPal, Authorize.net and 2Checkout on paid plans
  • Test mode that routes every gateway to its sandbox
  • Customer account page with downloads, licenses and subscriptions

Products, price options and bundles

  • Unlimited products
  • Several price options per product, such as Single Site, 5 Sites and Unlimited, or monthly and yearly
  • Files and license seats set per price option
  • Bundles that show their contents and the saving; buyers own each included product
  • Optional limit on total sales for limited runs

File downloads

  • Signed download links tied to the order, product and file
  • Links expire after one hour by default, and buyers can get a fresh link from their account
  • Optional access period after purchase and download limit
  • Download log
  • Files you sold are never deleted when the plugin is uninstalled

Subscriptions

  • Recurring price options billed every set number of days, weeks, months or years
  • Free trials
  • Fixed-term plans with a set number of charges, or billing until canceled
  • Upgrades charged at once with proration; downgrades scheduled for the next renewal
  • Grace period after a failed payment
  • Downloads and restricted content stop when a subscription lapses

Growing revenue

Discount codes

  • Percentage or fixed-amount discounts
  • Limited to specific products
  • Start and end dates
  • Total usage limit and per-customer limit

Abandoned cart recovery

  • Emails buyers who left items in their cart
  • A signed link that restores the cart
  • A sequence of emails, each with its own delay
  • Optional discount code in a recovery email
  • Stops after the number of days you set

Reviews

  • Verified-buyer status for reviewers who bought the product
  • Moderation, with optional automatic approval
  • Ratings, reviews and review form as shortcodes, a block and an Elementor widget

AI product recommendations (optional)

  • Related-product suggestions from the Anthropic Claude API
  • Off until you add an API key
  • A monthly call cap as a spending ceiling
  • Sends your catalog and the current cart, never names, email addresses or payment details

Selling internationally

Multi-currency

  • Automatic exchange rates from a configurable provider
  • Currency detected from the visitor's location, with a currency switcher
  • Fixed prices per currency for any price option
  • Price rounding options

EU VAT and invoices

  • VAT rates per EU country and tax classes per product
  • VIES validation and reverse charge
  • Location evidence stored with each order
  • Sequential invoices and credit notes
  • VAT report per country

EU VAT in detail

Software and paid content

Software licensing

  • License keys with activation limits per price option
  • Free activations for local and staging sites
  • Update server and a drop-in updater for plugins and themes
  • Customers free seats from their account

Software licensing

Paywall and memberships

  • Restrict posts, pages or parts of them to buyers
  • Memberships through subscription products
  • Restricted text kept out of REST, feeds and SEO descriptions
  • Paywalled-content structured data

Paywall and memberships

Running the store

Reports and exports

  • Revenue, product, gateway and VAT reports
  • CSV exports
  • Order, customer, subscription and license screens

Privacy and data

  • GDPR export and erasure through WordPress's privacy tools
  • Erasure anonymizes personal data and keeps the financial records tax law requires
  • Suggested privacy policy text for the services you switch on
  • Store data is kept on uninstall unless you choose to delete it
