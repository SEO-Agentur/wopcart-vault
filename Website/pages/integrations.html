Payment gateways

The free plugin settles through WopCart's Stripe Connect platform. WopCart Direct swaps that for your own accounts.

In the free plugin

  • Stripe — hosted Checkout, SCA ready, charged to your own connected Stripe account with 2% retained
  • Apple Pay — on-page wallet button, settled through Stripe; needs HTTPS and a domain registered with Stripe once

With WopCart Direct, your own accounts

  • Stripe and Apple Pay through your own Stripe account, 0% fee
  • PayPal
  • Mollie — iDEAL, Bancontact and the other Mollie methods
  • Authorize.net — on-page card form with Accept.js and recurring billing
  • 2Checkout

PayPal, Mollie, Authorize.net and 2Checkout offer no way to collect a platform fee, which is why they need your own credentials. Physical products can only be paid with gateways that support shipping.

Automation

Zapier, webhooks, Uncanny Automator

One event catalogue feeds every outbound channel, so a new event reaches Zapier, Automator and your own endpoints at the same time, under the same name. Signed webhooks carry an HMAC over a timestamp and the body, so your endpoint can verify the delivery came from your store.

Events

  • Orders: created, completed, failed, refunded
  • Subscriptions: created, renewed, renewal failed, cancelled, plan changed
  • Licences: created, activated, deactivated, expired, upgraded, transferred
  • Invoices issued and credit notes
  • Reviews, abandoned and recovered carts, form submissions, memberships

Hub

Services you can switch on

The integrations hub covers email marketing, CRM, automation, messaging, LMS, membership, affiliate and analytics services. Each one is off until you add its credentials.

  • LMS access for LearnDash, Tutor LMS and LifterLMS
  • Memberships with WordPress roles and MemberPress compatibility
  • Affiliate tracking through AffiliateWP and SliceWP, which calculate your partners' commissions
  • InvoicesGenerator for hosted invoice PDFs, while WopCart keeps the numbering
  • Anthropic's Claude API for optional product recommendations, off until you add a key

SEO

Yoast, Rank Math, AIOSEO, SEO-WP

  • Adds Product, review and paywall markup to the SEO plugin's own schema graph instead of a second one
  • Stops generated descriptions from exposing restricted text
  • Sets checkout, confirmation and account pages to noindex and removes them from the sitemap

Code

REST API

Authenticated with API keys. Keys are stored hashed and shown once, when you create them.

WP-CLI
