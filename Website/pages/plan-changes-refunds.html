Plan changes, domains and refunds

How WopCart plans handle upgrades, downgrades, domains and the EU right of withdrawal, in plain language.

This summary does not replace our Terms of Service and is not legal advice. If the two differ, the Terms apply.

In short

  • Yearly upgrades take effect immediately. The prorated difference is charged at once.
  • Lifetime upgrades cost the difference between the two lifetime prices.
  • Downgrades of a yearly plan take effect at your next renewal. You keep your current plan until then.
  • Plan changes are not refunded or credited.
  • Local and staging sites do not use a domain slot, up to 3 per key by default.
  • You can withdraw from any plan purchase, yearly or lifetime, within 14 days for a full refund. Your plan key is then revoked and your sites return to the free plan.

Upgrading

Yearly plans. When you move to a yearly plan with more domains, the change takes effect immediately, and we charge the prorated difference for the rest of your current year at once.

Example: you move from 1 site ($50 per year) to 10 sites ($99 per year) halfway through your year. The yearly difference is $49, so about $24.50 is charged when you upgrade. The exact amount depends on the days remaining.

Lifetime plans. When you move to a lifetime plan with more domains, the change takes effect immediately, and you pay the difference between the two lifetime prices. Example: from 1 site ($80) to 10 sites ($150), you pay $70.

Downgrading

When you move a yearly plan to a plan with fewer domains, the change takes effect at your next renewal. Until then, you keep the plan you paid for. From the renewal, you are billed the lower price.

If more domains are active than the new plan allows, you choose which sites stay. If you do not choose, the oldest activations are kept. You can withdraw a scheduled downgrade before the renewal date.

No refunds or credits for plan changes

Plan changes never result in a refund or account credit. An upgrade charges only the difference, and a downgrade of a yearly plan waits until the year you already paid for has ended.

When a plan ends

A cancelled yearly plan ends when the year you paid for is over. A lifetime plan does not expire. After a paid plan ends, the store returns to the free plan. Every feature keeps working, and payments are routed through WopCart's Stripe Connect platform with the 2% fee instead of your own gateway accounts.

Domains and staging sites

  • Each production domain where the plan key is saved uses one domain slot.
  • Local and staging sites do not use a slot. By default, up to 3 of them can be active on one key at the same time.
  • Local sites include localhost, private IP addresses and addresses ending in .local or .test. Staging sites include subdomains such as staging., dev. or test. and common hosting staging domains.
  • You can remove a domain in your wopcart.com account to free its slot and use the key on another site.
  • Removing the plan key from a site's settings also releases that site's slot.
  • If wopcart.com cannot be reached, a site on a paid plan keeps its plan for up to 14 days.

14-day right of withdrawal

Every customer can withdraw from the purchase of a paid plan, yearly or lifetime, within 14 days, without giving a reason. This applies to consumers and businesses, and even after you have received and activated your plan key.

To withdraw, email info@wopcart.com with your order number. We refund the full amount within 14 days to the payment method you used. At the same time, we revoke your plan key, and all sites that use it return to the free plan. Your sites and store data are not affected. After the 14 days, paid plans are not refunded.

This does not affect your statutory rights, for example if the product is faulty or not as described.
