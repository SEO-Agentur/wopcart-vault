Changes, domains, refunds

The rules, in plain language.

How WopCart Direct handles upgrades, downgrades, domain slots and the EU right of withdrawal.

This summary does not replace our Terms of Service and is not legal advice. Where the two differ, the Terms apply.

In short

Six sentences that cover almost every case.

  • There are no upgrades or plan changes. More sites, or a licence that never expires, is another licence, bought at the price on the pricing page.
  • Your existing licence is unaffected: it keeps running until it expires or you cancel it.
  • Need fewer sites? Cancel the licence you no longer want; it runs to the end of the period you paid for.
  • Nothing is prorated and nothing is credited, so the price you are shown is the price you pay.
  • Local and staging sites use no domain slot, up to 3 per key by default.
  • You can withdraw from any purchase within 14 days for a full refund. The key is then revoked and your sites return to the free 2% routing.

Wanting more

Buy it, the same way you bought the first one

There is no upgrade path, and that is deliberate. Working out what you already paid and how much of your year is left produces a different number for every customer — a figure we cannot print on a page, cannot quote you in an email and would have to unpick on every refund. So we do not do it.

Need ten sites instead of one? Buy the ten-site licence. Want a key that never expires? Buy the lifetime licence. Need another hundred sites next year? Buy another licence. Each one costs what the pricing page says it costs, before you click anything.

Your existing licence is not touched. It keeps working on the sites it is already on until it expires or you cancel it, and the new one covers whatever you point it at. Two keys are not a problem: the plugin reads them independently, and your account lists both.

Wanting less

Cancel the licence you no longer need. It runs to the end of the period you have already paid for and is not renewed. Nothing is refunded for the unused part, and nothing needs to be: you keep what you bought until it runs out.

Inside the first fourteen days a purchase is a different matter entirely — see the right of withdrawal below, which gives you the whole amount back.

Ending

What happens to your store

A cancelled yearly licence ends when the year you paid for is over. A lifetime licence does not expire. Your own gateways keep charging while the add-on is installed; an expired licence stops only the add-on's updates.

Remove the add-on and the store returns to the free routing: every feature keeps working, and payments are settled through WopCart's Stripe Connect platform with the 2% fee instead of your own accounts. Your products, orders, invoices and customer licences are not affected.

Domains

Slots, staging and local

  • Each production domain where the licence key is saved uses one slot.
  • Local and staging sites use no slot. By default, up to 3 of them can be active on one key at the same time.
  • Local means localhost, private IP addresses and addresses ending in .local or .test. Staging means subdomains such as staging., dev. or test., and the common hosting staging domains.
  • Remove a domain in your wopcart.com account to free its slot, or remove the key from that site's settings.
  • If wopcart.com cannot be reached, a licensed site keeps its licence for up to 14 days, so an outage on our side never puts a fee back on your sales.

14-day right of withdrawal

For consumers and businesses alike, and even after the key has been activated.

How to withdraw

Email info@wopcart.com with your order number, within 14 days of the purchase. No reason needed.

We refund the full amount within 14 days, to the payment method you used.

What happens then

The licence key is revoked, and every site using it returns to the free 2% routing. Your sites and store data are untouched. After the 14 days, purchases are not refunded.

This does not affect your statutory rights, for example if the product is faulty or not as described.
