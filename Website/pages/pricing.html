Domains

How slots are counted

  • Each production domain where the licence key is saved uses one domain slot.
  • Local and staging sites use no slot. By default, up to 3 of them can be active on one key at the same time.
  • Local means localhost, private IP addresses and addresses ending in .local or .test. Staging means subdomains such as staging., dev. or test., and the common hosting staging domains.
  • Remove a domain in your wopcart.com account to free its slot, or remove the key from that site's settings.

Changes

Moving between sizes

  • Yearly upgrades take effect immediately; the prorated difference is charged at once.
  • Lifetime upgrades cost the difference between the two lifetime prices, for example $70 from 1 site to 10 sites.
  • Downgrades of a yearly licence take effect at the next renewal. You keep what you paid for until then.
  • Changes are never refunded or credited. Read the details.

Pricing questions

Are any features locked in the free plugin?

No. There are no locked features and no limits on products or orders. The add-on changes only how payments are routed, and who holds the gateway accounts.

Why only Stripe and Apple Pay in the free plugin?

PayPal, Authorize.net, Mollie and 2Checkout offer no way to collect a platform fee. They need your own credentials, which is what WopCart Direct adds.

Yearly or lifetime?

Yearly costs less up front and can be cancelled anytime. From two years on, lifetime is cheaper at every size: 1 site is $100 over two yearly payments against $80 once.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 14 days of any purchase, yearly or lifetime. You get the full amount back, the licence key is revoked and your sites fall back to the 2% routing. Size changes are not refunded.

What happens when the licence ends?

Your own gateways keep working; only updates for the add-on stop. Remove the add-on and the store returns to the free routing with the 2% fee. Your products, orders and licences are untouched either way.

Is a licence key needed for the free plugin?

No. The free plugin asks for no key and checks nothing with us except the payment settlement itself. Only WopCart Direct carries a key, for its updates and its domain count.
