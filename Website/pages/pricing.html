WopCart pricing

Every feature is in the free plugin. You choose how payments are routed: through WopCart at 2% per sale, or through your own gateway accounts at 0%.

  • Yearly: renews automatically each year. Cancel anytime; your plan runs to the end of the year you paid for.
  • Lifetime: one payment, no renewals. The plan key never expires and includes updates and the plan check for as long as WopCart is offered. "Lifetime" refers to the lifetime of the WopCart service, not the buyer's life.

Prices are in US dollars. For consumers in the EU, VAT at their country's rate applies, and EU prices at checkout include VAT. Business customers in the EU with a valid VAT number and customers outside the EU pay no VAT.

How domains are counted

  • Each production domain where the plan key is saved uses one domain slot.
  • Local and staging sites do not use a slot. By default, up to 3 of them can be active on one key at the same time.
  • Local sites include localhost, private IP addresses and addresses ending in .local or .test. Staging sites include subdomains such as staging., dev. or test. and common hosting staging domains.
  • You can remove a domain in your wopcart.com account to free its slot and use the key on another site.
  • Removing the plan key from a site's settings also releases that site's slot.

Changing plans

  • Yearly upgrades take effect immediately. The prorated difference is charged at once.
  • Lifetime upgrades cost the difference between the two lifetime prices, for example $70 from 1 site to 10 sites.
  • Downgrades of a yearly plan take effect at your next renewal. You keep your current plan until then.
  • Plan changes are not refunded or credited.

What you pay in total

Free plan: the 2% WopCart platform fee plus Stripe's processing fees.

Paid plan: the yearly price, or the one-time lifetime price, plus your payment provider's processing fees. WopCart takes nothing from your transactions.

Pricing questions

Are any features locked on the free plan?

No. There are no locked features and no limits on products. The plan only decides how payments are routed.

Why is the free plan limited to Stripe and Apple Pay?

PayPal, Authorize.net and 2Checkout provide no way to collect a platform fee, so they are available on paid plans, where you use your own credentials.

Yearly or lifetime: which costs less?

Yearly costs less up front and you can cancel anytime. If you use WopCart for two years or more, the lifetime plan costs less on every tier: for example, 1 site is $100 over two yearly payments or $80 once.

Can I convert a yearly plan to lifetime?

Converting a yearly plan to a lifetime plan is coming soon.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 14 days of any plan purchase, yearly or lifetime. You get a full refund, your plan key is revoked and your sites return to the free plan. Plan changes are not refunded or credited.

What happens when my plan ends?

A cancelled yearly plan ends when the paid year is over. A lifetime plan does not expire. When a plan ends, the store returns to the free plan: features keep working, and payments are routed through WopCart's Stripe Connect platform with the 2% fee.

What if the plan check cannot reach wopcart.com?

A site on a paid plan keeps its plan for up to 14 days while wopcart.com is unreachable, so an outage on our side does not add a fee to your sales.
