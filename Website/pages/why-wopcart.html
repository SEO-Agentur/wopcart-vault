When another tool fits better

Four cases where we would not recommend WopCart.

You need a marketplace

Several vendors selling on one store, with commission payouts. WopCart has no multi-vendor mode and none is planned.

You need carrier labels

WopCart ships physical goods with its own zones and rates, but it does not yet buy labels or pull live rates from DHL, UPS or FedEx. That is on the roadmap.

You want native mailing-list apps

WopCart has an integrations hub for email, CRM and automation services, and reaches the rest through Zapier or signed webhooks rather than a dedicated add-on per service.

You want 0% without paying anything

The free plugin carries a 2% platform fee, because it settles through our Stripe Connect platform. Removing the fee means buying WopCart Direct.
