Domains

How slots are counted

  • Each production domain where the key is saved uses one slot.
  • Local means localhost, private IP addresses and addresses ending in .local or .test. Staging means subdomains such as staging., dev. or test., and the common hosting staging domains.
  • Free a slot by removing the domain in your account, or by removing the key from that site's settings.

Fine print

Renewal, refund, expiry

  • Yearly renews automatically. Cancel anytime and it runs to the end of the year you paid for.
  • One-time never renews and includes updates for as long as WopCart is offered.
  • 14 days to withdraw from either, for a full refund. The key is then revoked and your sites fall back to the 2% routing.
  • If the licence lapses, your own gateways keep charging. Only add-on updates stop — we never put a fee back on your sales behind your back.
  • Sizes can be changed anytime; changes are not refunded or credited. Details.
