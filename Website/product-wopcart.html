What a WopCart plan unlocks

  • 0% WopCart platform fee on every transaction, instead of 2% on the free plan
  • Your own gateway accounts: Stripe, PayPal, Authorize.net and 2Checkout, plus Apple Pay through your own Stripe account
  • Money goes from your buyer to your gateway to you, with WopCart not in the payment flow
  • The same features as the free plugin: no extra download and no second plugin to install

After purchase, you receive a plan key. Paste it under WopCart → Settings → Payments → Plan key on each site. See the getting started guide.

Plans

Every plan is available yearly or as a one-time lifetime plan, in US dollars, and includes every feature. Plans differ only in the number of production domains. EU consumers pay VAT on top at checkout.

How domains are counted

  • Each production domain where the plan key is saved uses one domain slot.
  • Local and staging sites do not use a slot. By default, up to 3 of them can be active on one key at the same time.
  • Local sites include localhost, private IP addresses and addresses ending in .local or .test. Staging sites include subdomains such as staging., dev. or test. and common hosting staging domains.
  • You can remove a domain in your wopcart.com account to free its slot and use the key on another site.
  • Removing the plan key from a site's settings also releases that site's slot.

Plan changes

  • Yearly upgrades take effect immediately. The prorated difference is charged at once.
  • Lifetime upgrades cost the difference between the two lifetime prices.
  • Downgrades of a yearly plan take effect at your next renewal. You keep your current plan until then.
  • Plan changes are not refunded or credited.

Details: plan changes, domains and refunds.

Immediate delivery

Your plan key is delivered right after payment. You can still withdraw from the purchase, yearly or lifetime, within 14 days for a full refund. The plan key is then revoked and your sites return to the free plan.

If your plan ends

A yearly plan ends if you cancel it, at the end of the year you paid for. A lifetime plan does not expire. When a plan ends, your store returns to the free plan: every feature keeps working, and payments are routed through WopCart's Stripe Connect platform with the 2% fee.
