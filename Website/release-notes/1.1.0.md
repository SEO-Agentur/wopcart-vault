# WopCart 1.1.0 – Release-Entwurf (noch nicht veröffentlicht)

Wird beim nächsten Plugin-Deploy auf wopcart.com übernommen: `wopcart.php` (Header + `const VERSION`), `readme.txt` (Stable tag + Changelog), Seite `/changelog/` (neuer Eintrag `id="1-1-0"`, „Unreleased“ aktualisieren), Seite `/roadmap/` (Shipped / In progress / Planned). Nur aufnehmen, was tatsächlich im Build ist und die Live-Prüfung bestanden hat.

## Added
- **Payment forms:** block-based form builder, 17 templates (payments, donations, sign-ups, events, surveys, contact, bookings, pay what you want), fixed/tiered/custom amounts, one-time or recurring, goals with progress bars, entries with CSV export, block and shortcode.
- **Integrations hub:** cards with on/off switches and settings for Mailchimp, Brevo, MailerLite, ActiveCampaign, FluentCRM, Zapier, Make, n8n, Pabbly Connect, Integrately, Uncanny Automator, WPFunnels, Slack, Discord, Telegram, Twilio SMS, Google Sheets, Fluent Support, FluentCommunity, LearnDash, Tutor LMS, LifterLMS, Wishlist Member, Restrict Content Pro, Paid Memberships Pro, AffiliateWP, SliceWP, Google Analytics 4 and Meta Conversions API; consent-aware, with retries.
- **Memberships:** levels with WordPress roles, access rules per page, block and menu item, member shortcodes, MemberPress compatibility, configurable behaviour when a subscription is paused.
- **Invoices:** EU-compliant invoice content, payment links with copy/open, resend, void via cancellation credit note, activity history, reminders before/on/after the due date, 6-year retention lock.
- **Cart page** and a setting for where buyers go after adding to cart (default: checkout).
- **Multilingual:** WPML and Polylang support.
- **Page builders:** Divi modules, Bricks elements, more Elementor widgets (payment form, account, courses).
- **Design:** new admin dashboard (period comparison, revenue chart) and redesigned checkout, cart and confirmation; checkout layout and accent colour settings.

## Changed
- Refund requests are only auto-granted when nothing was refunded or declined before.
- Physical products can only be paid with gateways that support shipping (Stripe for now).
- Requires PHP 8.2 or newer.

## Fixed
- Changing a yearly plan could fail with an error.
- Invoice numbers, refunds on orders without an invoice, and credit notes.
- Several security hardening fixes (permissions, webhook address checks, data in retry queues).
